## Why this document exists

OGR Suite is released under the **AGPL-3.0-or-later**, which guarantees
that the program stays free: anyone may use it, including for paid
engineering work, and anyone who offers a *modified* version as a network
service must publish their source.

The maintainer additionally wishes to keep the option of offering a
**hosted service** under separate commercial terms. As the sole copyright
holder of the original code, he may do that with his own work: a licence
constrains what *others* may do with the code, not what its author may do
with it.

That option disappears the moment outside contributions arrive without an
agreement. A patch you write is **your** copyright, licensed to the
project under the AGPL — so the combined work could no longer be offered
under any other terms, because relicensing someone else's AGPL code is
precisely what the AGPL forbids.

This CLA solves that without taking anything away from you.

6. **No warranty is expected of you.** Contributions are provided as-is;

## What this does NOT mean

- It does **not** make the project closed source. The AGPL version
  remains, and every contribution is part of it.
- It does **not** let the maintainer remove your name. Authorship is
  recorded in the version history.
- It does **not** transfer your copyright.

## How to accept

Add a line to `CONTRIBUTORS.md` in the same pull request:

```
- Your Name <email or GitHub handle> — I agree to the CLA in CLA.md
```

and sign your commits off with the Developer Certificate of Origin:

```
git commit -s -m "your message"
```

which appends a `Signed-off-by:` trailer.

For contributions of more than a few lines the maintainer may ask for
written confirmation by e-mail before merging.

## Questions

If any of this is unclear, or if you would rather contribute **without**
the relicensing grant, open an issue and say so. A contribution offered
under the AGPL alone is still welcome — it simply cannot be included in a
commercially licensed build, and will be marked as such so the boundary
stays documented.
